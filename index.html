    Questions

    Before You Join

    Do you guarantee I will make money?

    No, and be careful of anyone in this industry who does. Trading carries real risk and the majority of retail traders lose money. What a membership gets you is a defined strategy, the tools to run it, structured training and people who trade it every session. What you do with that is down to your own discipline, risk management and execution.

    Is this financial advice?

    No. Everything here is educational. We do not manage money, place trades on your behalf, or tell you what to buy or sell. Nothing on this site or in the community should be treated as a personal recommendation. If you need advice about your own circumstances, speak to a regulated financial adviser.

    What returns should I expect?

    We do not publish return targets, because any honest answer depends on your account size, your risk per trade, how consistently you follow the rules and what the market is doing. Anyone promising you a monthly percentage is guessing or selling.

    Do I need experience to start?

    No. The course starts at the foundations and the Trading Tools and Setup module walks you through getting your platform working before you place anything. Start on a demo account, which is exactly what the first tool lesson covers.

    What do I need to trade this?

    A TradingView account for charting and a broker account on MetaTrader 5 or TradeLocker for execution. The setup lessons walk through both, including opening a free demo account first.

    How much time does this take each day?

    It is a scalping strategy, so it is session based rather than all day. Programmable alerts mean you are not watching charts waiting for a setup to appear.

    Do I need a large account to start?

    No, and you should not start with real money at all until the strategy is working on demo. The Prop Firm Strategy module also covers trading funded capital, which is an option once you can trade consistently.

    Can I cancel?

    Yes. It is $77 per month and you can cancel at any time. You keep access for the period you have paid for.
